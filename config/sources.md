# Preferred Research Sources

## Tier 1 — official / primary

- NFL and team injury reports
- Official team transaction announcements
- Direct coach/player press-conference comments
- Official depth-chart and inactive announcements

## Tier 2 — reliable reporting

Use established NFL news reporters and team beat reporters for role, injury, practice, and snap-limit context. Treat attributed reporting as reporting, not as official fact unless confirmed by the team/NFL.

## Tier 3 — projections / data / analysis

Preferred examples:
- FantasyPros
- Action Network / Sean Koerner (@The_Oddsmaker)
- Chris Raybon (@ChrisRaybon)
- Fantasy Points Data / Ryan Heath (@RyanJ_Heath)
- Establish The Run / Evan Silva / Adam Levitan
- JJ Zachariason (@LateRoundQB)
- RotoGrinders and other established betting/fantasy data sources when methodology is transparent

No single expert is authoritative. Track timestamped signals and compare them with independent projections and the market.

## Grok / X intelligence

A Grok/X-style feed should prioritize:
- official team accounts
- beat reporters
- injury and transactions reporters
- established projection/data analysts
- prop/betting analysts with transparent reasoning

The feed should avoid treating retweets, anonymous screenshots, tout records, or unsupported social-media claims as facts.

Each signal should include:
- source / handle
- timestamp
- player/game
- signal type
- classification (`confirmed_fact`, `projection`, `analyst_opinion`, `speculation`)
- concise claim
- source URL when available
- whether verified elsewhere
- impact on prop thesis

## Sportsbook / market sources

Capture the exact sportsbook, line, odds, and timestamp. When possible capture both sides of a market so no-vig probability can be calculated. Do not merge numbers from different sportsbooks into a fictional single market.
