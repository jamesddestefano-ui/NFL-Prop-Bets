## Lessons (with evidence classification) and PRIME challenges

**1. VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE — Probability must be compared to price.** The Unabated video offers the hypothetical 50%-probability coin flip at +110: EV per $1 = .5*1.10-.5*1 = +$0.05 (5% expected ROI). At -110 the identical proposition has negative EV. The video's broader claims of abundant market edges are **analyst opinion**, not verified achieved returns. **METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** PRIME's 100-point rubric must never outweigh conservative EV at the *current executable* sportsbook line. The controlling framework already requires this; enforce rather than revise.

**2. VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE plus MODEL UNCERTAINTY — No-vig methods can disagree.** Hypothetical SAME-book/SAME-threshold over -125, under +105: raw implied probabilities are 55.556% and 48.780%, totaling 104.336%. Multiplicative fair Over=53.247%; additive=53.388%; power≈53.459%. At hypothetical -200/+150: multiplicative Over=62.500% versus power≈63.792%. The de-vig probabilities remain *market-derived estimates*, not ground truth. At hypothetical offered -110 and p=53.247%, modeled EV≈+1.653%; a 2-percentage-point conservative adjustment gives p=51.247% and EV≈-2.165%. **MODEL_UNCERTAINTY:** record method spread and whether conservative EV changes sign rather than cherry-pick a method. Technical clarification: -110/-110 represents 4.762 *percentage points overround*; 4.545% is the expected equal-stake hold, a different denominator.

**3. EMPIRICAL CLAIM / UNTESTED NFL HYPOTHESIS — No permanently sharp prop bookmaker.** Video ~02:57-04:24 and Unabated article suggest using market makers to spot delayed bookmaker repricing. The identity of an efficient book can vary by market, timing and liquidity. **PRICE_CONFLICT:** for NFL props backtest which timestamp-aligned bookmaker/reference consensus best predicts the subsequent *same-threshold* closing benchmark, tracking misses and passes. Different thresholds require a distribution; no imaginary consensus price across unmatched thresholds.

**4. MATHEMATICS + ANALYST OPINION — Kelly sizing is vulnerable to p error.** Formula for theoretical binary Kelly fraction: f*=(p*d-1)/(d-1), truncated at zero. At hypothetical p=.55, -110 decimal d≈1.9091, theoretical full Kelly≈5.5% of bankroll, quarter Kelly≈1.375%. At p=.52 the same bet has negative EV and zero theoretical Kelly stake. The video suggests quarter Kelly, but this is not a mandate: probabilities, correlation, bankroll limits and uncertainty remain essential. **MODEL_UNCERTAINTY:** test sensitivity of sizing to conservative p; do not prescribe aggressive sizing from an uncalibrated estimate.

**5. MATHEMATICS / RESEARCH PROPOSAL — CLV and parlays need probability-matched reference.** A hypothetical bet +110 with a -110/-110 matched-threshold close has no-vig closing p=.50 and price CLV expressed as closing-value expected ROI of 2.10*.50-1=+5%. A different closing threshold cannot be converted to price CLV without a player outcome distribution. A hypothetical +261 two-leg parlay (decimal 3.61) needs joint probability >27.70%; if independent legs are each 55%, joint=.3025 and EV≈+9.20%; if each 52%, joint=.2704 and EV≈-2.39%. If P(A)=.55 but P(B|A)=.50, joint=.275 and EV≈-0.73%, illustrating correlation risk. **METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** require a defensible conservative joint p rather than combining two attractive standalone props. These are fabricated *educational examples*, not actual or executable bets.

**6. EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE limited to other sport — Probability calibration is more useful than raw accuracy alone.** Proper scores such as Brier=(forecast p - realized y)^2 reward honest probability forecasts; the Walsh/Joshi paper supports testing calibration-based model selection but cannot establish any NFL-specific EV advantage. **METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** precommit p before game, report Brier and reliability by market and timing bucket, with out-of-sample validation and error bars. Do not infer model skill from a streak of winning legs.

Reject if method differences negligible or no persistent calibration advantage.
2. **CLV reference quality:** archive open, signal, recommendation and closing *same market/threshold* quotes from identified books. Reject if apparent advantage vanishes after quote-freshness controls.
3. **Parlay joint-probability calibration:** compare independent products against conditional/game-state simulation on held-out weeks; grade leg scores separately from joint ticket outcome and use the actual combined price. Reject if joint forecasts or Brier do not improve out of sample.
