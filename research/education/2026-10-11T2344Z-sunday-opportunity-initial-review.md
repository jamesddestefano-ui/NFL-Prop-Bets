## Completed facts and carefully bounded interpretations

### Allen: injury-contingent volume

**EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE (confirmed official facts):** The Jets beat Cleveland 17–12. Braelon Allen recorded 24 carries for 116 yards and one rushing touchdown, plus one catch for zero yards. Isaiah Davis recorded two carries for eight yards. Breece Hall was officially inactive. Allen's official prior-week log lists 14 carries for 59 yards. [Official final box](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/game-day/2026/reg-week5/browns-at-jets/box-score), [Allen log](https://www.newyorkjets.com/team/players-roster/braelon-allen/logs/), [Jets inactives](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/jets-vs-browns-game-inactives-10-11-2026).

**ANALYST OPINION (conditional interpretation):** This establishes substantial realized volume in Hall's absence. It does not establish a permanent takeover, a future snap share, or the workload Allen would receive with Hall active. A prior-week count comparison is descriptive; the health and game environments differ.

**Next evidence needed:** Hall's subsequent official status, complete snap/route counts, backfield situational assignments, game script and a comparable healthy sample. A touchdown alone cannot supply goal-line share.

### Garrett Wilson: production concentration is not target share

**EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE (confirmed official facts):** Wilson recorded seven receptions for 104 yards and one touchdown. Geno Smith finished 17/30 for 182 passing yards. The official recap reports 70 Wilson receiving yards on the opening possession. [Final box](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/game-day/2026/reg-week5/browns-at-jets/box-score), [Jets recap](https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/jets-vs-browns-nfl-game-recap-green-and-white-edge-cleveland-10-11-2026).

**VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE (arithmetic only):** 104 / 182 × 100 = 57.1% of team passing yards. This is receiving-production concentration against gross passing yards, not target share, route participation, TPRR, first-read share or air-yard share. Neither targets nor routes were verified. A concentrated yardage result may reflect a few productive plays rather than a persistent opportunity change.
