# PRIME — offline decision-policy evaluation (2026-10-13)

Study-only finding: a return calculated from selected, placed tickets is not automatically an estimate of what another selection policy would have achieved. Frozen candidate contracts, predecision prices, source times, graded results, and passes are needed for a defensible shadow replay. No actionable market quotes, confirmed fills, or new rules are asserted.

Primary original-source reading: Dudik, Langford and Li (2011), https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4601 ; Li, Chu, Langford and Wang (2011), https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.5956 . Their contextual-bandit evidence is not NFL-specific. A deterministic old policy may have no support for a proposed new action; inverse-propensity evaluation is then unidentified. With complete authentic contemporaneous offer and grading, a prospective hypothetical replay can score both selected and passed offers without placing wagers.

The independent educational experiment and complete source notes were prepared in this run, but no historical source, cash records or methodology was changed.

## Synthetic illustration (not NFL data)

Four hypothetical independent offers at -110 settle WIN, WIN, LOSS, LOSS. The logged selection policy bets only the first two and shows +20/11 units total and +90.91% return per unit risked. An alternative policy betting all four yields -2/11 units and -4.55% per unit risked. These figures are invented to demonstrate that comparing different action sets requires a frozen opportunity universe. Without the two passed candidates' original contract quotes and graded outcomes, the broader policy cannot be retrospectively scored. A policy with zero bets has no defined ROI per risked unit.

Research tests to propose: timestamp-aligned complete contract capture at fixed checkpoints, prospective dual-policy shadow scoring, game/week clustered uncertainty, and explicit missing-data denominators. The known 6 decision, 5 ticket, and 3 pass CSV width anomalies prevent unreviewed use of the current ledgers for these tests. No changes to historical records, sizing, or decision gates are warranted.
