## Independent methodological critique

**METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** a prompt is a research procedure specification, not an estimated statistical model. A probability needs an auditable mapping from as-of role/opportunity inputs to an outcome distribution at an exact threshold, and a calibration procedure fitted without future outcomes. A fluent rationale, persona, or confidence label cannot supply that mapping. Even a formally correct EV calculation can be wrong economically when its probability is unsupported.

**DATA_CONFLICT risk:** asking a model to retrieve current facts does not establish freshness or source access. Preserve original URLs, publication/update times, observation times, named book, jurisdiction, exact contract and opposing prices. Unknown data remain null. A repeated model response or a second chatbot relying on the same source is not independent confirmation. Official participation estimates must not become observed practices, and projection means must not become clearing probabilities.

**MODEL_UNCERTAINTY:** evaluate central and adverse probability cases separately. An uncertainty range chosen to make a wager qualify is not conservative validation. A market-aware forecast can be useful, but copying market probability and then treating agreement as independent model support double-counts evidence. Separate p_market, p_model_raw, p_internal and p_conservative.

**UNVERIFIED_CLAIM:** short-run performance needs the complete precommitted selection set, probabilities, prices, stakes convention, settlement rules, outcomes and missingness before it can be audited. Wins alone do not establish positive expected return. Prompt revisions chosen after seeing results must be evaluated on a new chronological holdout. No source-profit ranking follows from this review.

Counterargument: a structured prompt can improve coverage, make missing data explicit and reduce transcription errors. That is a plausible operational benefit worth testing separately from forecasting skill. A failed probability model does not imply its research checklist is useless; a good checklist does not establish betting edge.

## Checked hypothetical arithmetic: price and uncertainty

**VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE; invented inputs only.** Consider a standard binary half-point NFL reception contract, assuming no push or void and decimal odds d = 1 + 100/110 = 21/11. This is not an observed sportsbook quote or a player recommendation.

- Break-even: 1/d = 11/21 = 52.380952%.
- If p = 0.55, expected ROI = p*d - 1 = 0.05 = +5.00%.
- If the plausible adverse p = 0.51, expected ROI = -0.026363636 = -2.636364%, approximately -2.64%.
- Central probability edge is only 0.55 - 11/21 = 2.619048 percentage points, below the framework's usual approximately four-point gate; conservative EV is negative. The +5% central ROI alone does not qualify the hypothetical candidate.
- Both-sided same-book, same-contract, same-time -110 prices would normalize to 50% each. With only one quoted side, that no-vig benchmark cannot be asserted.

These are conditional-on-the-assumptions arithmetic checks, not evidence that 55% or 51% is a defensible NFL forecast. Integer thresholds and special void/early-exit terms require their own outcome space and p_win*(d-1)-p_loss treatment.

## Falsifiable NFL test proposal, not an executed pilot

This is a narrow extension of [task004's candidate-universe design](../dot_reports/PRIME-DOT-20261013-004/2026-10-14-candidate-universe-manifest.md), task006's requested capture specification in the [queue](../dot_task_queue.jsonl), and the existing [October18 shadow-pilot plan](../pilots/2026-10-18/PLAN.md). It does not replace those plans or claim their completion.

**Hypothesis:** on identical frozen public NFL evidence packets, a structured prompt checklist reduces unsupported factual/probability assertions and increases complete source provenance relative to a simpler fixed checklist. A second, distinct hypothesis is that any resulting probability forecast improves held-out scoring over the same-contract no-vig market baseline. The operational and predictive hypotheses can have different outcomes.

1. Before capture, freeze prompt text/version, model/version and sampling settings, input packet digest, permitted tools, candidate universe, checkpoint times, adjudication rubric and rejection criteria. Use existing fixed reception/receiving-yard/rushing-yard scope. Retain all candidates and capture failures, including PASS/AVOID. No outcome-aware selection and no assumed fills.
2. Run both procedures on the exact same as-of packet; randomize presentation order for a reviewer blinded to procedure. If retrieval is included, use equal access and time budgets and separately report retrieval differences; that becomes a workflow test, not an isolated wording test. Record every run and predeclare one-run or repeated-run aggregation, rather than selecting the best answer.
3. Evaluate factual assertions against original evidence and their actual timestamps. Count unsupported assertions per response and per adjudicated assertion; also report assertion counts so silence cannot masquerade as completeness. Record required-field completion, correctly preserved missingness, source/contract errors, latency and abstention rate. A missing probability is an abstention, never 50% by default.
4. Operational pilot gate: zero fabricated book/line/price/source/probability-provenance fields and zero future-input admissions in the usable subset; report violations across all attempted records. Require reconciled candidate counts and an auditable citation trail. Any critical violation rejects operational readiness for that procedure; do not erase its record.
5. Only after capture readiness, freeze an adequately sized chronological evaluation window and practical-effect/precision targets using pilot game/week-cluster variability. Score forecasts on paired eligible contracts with the declared probability basis, proper Brier score and calibration; keep pushes/voids separate. Report paired coverage, abstentions and missing-outcome sensitivity. Fewer missing predictions is useful but is not automatically better calibration.
6. On independently available exact quotes, report hypothetical unit return and matched-contract LINE_CLV/PRICE_CLV, with complete denominators and game/week/thesis clustering. The close is a benchmark, not truth. No quote means priced replay unavailable; no close means CLV unavailable. An observed offer is not an accepted ticket.
7. Reject predictive superiority if the precommitted paired held-out improvement criterion fails, if uncertainty includes no practical benefit, or if conclusions reverse under predeclared missingness/availability sensitivity. Report INCONCLUSIVE when access or effective sample size cannot support the claim. Preserve failed hypotheses and all prompt revisions; revisions start a new future cohort.

Sunday's up-to-ten-contract pilot can expose capture failures but cannot establish calibrated probabilities or profitability. No test above was run in this review, no prospective price/outcome dataset was collected, and no method or stake policy is adopted.

## Confidence, scope and deduplication

High confidence in the explicit arithmetic and distinction between a procedure and validated predictions; moderate confidence in the proposed operational test; NFL predictive benefit unestablished.
