## Five lessons and actionable proposals

1. **Price-first conservative EV gate [verified arithmetic].** At -110 binary odds, hypothetical p=.55 gives +5% ROI; adverse p=.52 gives -0.73%. A prior hypothetical +258 parlay with stated 26–29% joint p spans -6.92% to +3.82% ROI. The old 'playable' conclusion contradicts positive *conservative* EV at the lower end; smaller stake does not repair negative ROI. Action: build a deterministic calculation-and-label consistency validator before any BET.
2. **Model the actual contract, not the mean [verified math, distribution is assumption].** Over 4.5 receptions requires P(R>=5); integer Over 50 may push. With a push, EV = p_win*(d-1)-p_loss = p_win*d+p_push-1. An illustrative -110 p_win=.50, p_push=.10, p_loss=.40 has +5.45% ROI whereas treating .50 as binary with no push incorrectly gives -4.55%. Test alternate-line monotonicity and correctly conditioned push calibration. Publisher video [Jack Andrews / Unabated, Profitable Prop Betting in 3 Easy Steps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oD4_mcRuHe4) via [transcript](https://unabated.com/post/profitable-prop-betting-in-3-easy-steps), publisher page dated 2026-07-29; original YouTube upload date and video timestamps not confirmed.
3. **Be critical of market reference estimates [verified identities; model assumptions].** Book pairs do not identify objective true probability, and different margin removal methods yield different results. Matched-threshold paired quotes and timestamp alignment are prerequisites; do not treat one market as universally sharp. Freeze reference-book panel and cutoff before measuring CLV. Interview video [Circles Off, Pizzola/Strober](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_XgQBjoATgc), YouTube metadata dated 2025-09-10; [publisher transcript](https://www.betstamp.com/education/the-secrets-to-becoming-a-successful-sports-betting-trader) has 19:50 fragmented props, 29:14–31:13 adverse selection, 56:42 calibration; timestamps are transcript markers, not playback verified.
4. **Evaluate probability quality on bets selected, not just all forecasts [verified counterexample; empirical NFL validation pending].** A synthetic model may be calibrated overall but wrong on its +EV-selected subset. Precommit forecasts for *all eligible* candidate markets including passes; separately grade all forecasts and selected ones by Brier, reliability, time, market and injury status using untouched future weeks. Lower Brier alone does not prove superior calibration. Source: [scikit-learn probability calibration](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html); [Cawley & Talbot JMLR 2010](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html).
