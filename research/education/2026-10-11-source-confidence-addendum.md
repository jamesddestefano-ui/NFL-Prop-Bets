## Three different judgments

- **Access/attribution:** confidence that the identified text was actually accessed and correctly attributed. This does not certify word-for-word agreement with unplayed audio.
- **Supported claims:** confidence in the narrow mathematical or methodological statements retained in the report, after its corrections and qualifications. This is not blanket confidence in everything the source says.
- **NFL transfer:** strength of evidence that the material improves NFL-prop forecasts, executable EV or results. General algebra can apply under stated assumptions without demonstrating an NFL advantage.

**High** means strong direct support for the stated, bounded claim. **Moderate** means useful support with meaningful access, interpretation, design or scope limitations. **Low** means insufficient support for a confident conclusion. Grades are qualitative judgments, not numerical confidence intervals, audit certifications, commercial endorsements or source-profitability rankings. “Low: untested” means absent relevant validation, not evidence that a method fails.

## How to use these grades

**VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE:** the package's independently reproduced identities and counterexamples have high confidence conditional on their inputs. Its invented probabilities, report likelihoods, dependence and quotes remain **MODEL ASSUMPTION**. High arithmetic confidence cannot upgrade those inputs.

Actual source/access observations and non-NFL studies are **EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE** only within their recorded scope. Practitioner interpretation remains **ANALYST OPINION**. Proposed NFL improvements remain **UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS**. Commercial ties and shared source families affect interpretation; neither affiliation nor the number of agreeing outlets proves skill or invalidity.

The useful conclusion is strong support for asking better, testable questions, with no demonstrated NFL forecasting or betting advantage from this session. Later timestamped, settlement-matched evidence could change a grade through another dated addendum. No source or model receives permanent authority.
