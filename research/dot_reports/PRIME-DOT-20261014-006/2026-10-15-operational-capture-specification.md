## Mathematical lesson and falsifiable improvement

**VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE:** with decimal price d and unconditional win/loss probabilities, expected net return is p_win(d−1)−p_loss. A no-vig consensus substitutes an estimated reference probability; the arithmetic does not make that estimate true.

**Synthetic NFL-reception example, no observed price:** suppose an Over4.5 reception quote pays decimal2.00. Its implied break-even is50%. A hypothetical same-book simultaneous Under at2.00 gives normalized no-vig50%. A separate frozen reference panel estimates55%, raw football model57%, calibrated/internal54%, conservative50%. Probability edge4percentage points and central expected ROI8% pass illustrative central gates, but conservative ROI0% fails a strictly positive conservative gate. Internal fair odds are decimal1/.54=1.851852 (approximately−117.39), conservative fair odds2.00 (+100). Under a5%ROI gate and4-point edge gate, strict conservative positivity requires d>2.00; equality is insufficient. Maximum playable line cannot be derived without probabilities at other thresholds;4.5 is only the evaluated threshold. No stake or wager follows.

**METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** if a reference panel blends the tested book with weightw, reference p=w*p_book+(1−w)*p_other. Its measured discrepancy from that book is (1−w)*(p_other−p_book). Synthetic p_book=.50,p_other=.60,w=.50 yields .55 versus leave-one-out .60. The blend is not independent corroboration; excluding the tested book changes the estimand and may increase noise. This is algebra, not proof either benchmark is better. A related future-close weighting scheme must be trained on earlier weeks; choosing weights using this Sunday's close leaks outcomes of the benchmark-selection process.

**UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS:** timestamp/identity gating plus a predeclared leave-one-book-out diagnostic reduces false apparent advantages caused by stale or self-referential benchmarks. Freeze equal-weight median baseline, eligible books, rule matching and missingness before capture. Compare full-panel and leave-one-out probabilities and decision disagreement on identical eligible contracts. Report both coverage and deviations, never select the version with prettier ROI. If matched panels cannot be formed, the test is INCONCLUSIVE. For predictive claims later, train weights chronologically, score paired held-out probabilities with proper Brier/calibration and game/week clustering, and require a predeclared practical improvement with uncertainty. Failure or inadequate sample is preserved. No NFL superiority experiment was run today.
