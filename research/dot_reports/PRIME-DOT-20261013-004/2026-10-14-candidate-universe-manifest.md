# PRIME-DOT-20261013-004 — Immutable candidate universe and honest shadow replay

Research: 2026-10-14 12:27–12:35 UTC / 08:27–08:35 EDT. NFL player props only. Review-only design, not a production ledger, adopted betting rule, or empirical NFL performance result.

## Answer and three lessons

1. **VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE:** complete observed contracts and independently graded outcomes permit hypothetical flat-unit payoff replay without placing wagers. They do not identify accepted fills, capacity, execution delays or actual profit. Ticket-only samples cannot identify a broader policy's return.
2. **MODEL ASSUMPTION / proposed design:** freeze the sampling frame before examining edges. “All candidates” must mean every eligible observation within named books, markets, games and collection windows, including unsuccessful capture attempts. It cannot mean only attractive props discovered by analysts.
3. **EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE, bounded repository audit:** the existing CSVs are not a complete replay panel. Preserve structural errors and missingness, rather than inventing joins or repairing history. A structurally valid row is not necessarily verified or semantically correct.

This operationalizes the October 13 offline-policy note and task 002 prospective protocol. It adds explicit units of observation, as-of admission, lifecycle events, payoff denominators and adversarial fixtures. It does not claim the older controls lacked these principles.

## Proposed research schema, not a competing truth system

A pilot would map to the existing ledgers and immutable archive; production adoption needs PRIME review. Keep observations and amendments append-only. Corrections name their superseded event; preserve original content. Hashes detect later content changes, not truthful collection time: retain contemporaneous repository commit/receipt evidence too.

**Unit and denominator definition:** one candidate is `(manifest_id, checkpoint_id, book_id, canonical_contract_id)`, with one policy decision per candidate. Repeated quote updates are evidence for that candidate, not additional candidates. Across checkpoints, selecting the same economic contract again is forbidden in the pilot unless a repeat-betting rule was precommitted; the policy uses a first-eligible-entry rule, retains later observations, and records them as already evaluated/selected rather than silently duplicating stakes. Cross-book alternatives stay visible but one-contract selection across books follows a precommitted best-eligible-price tie-break, not hindsight. Repeated outcomes remain clustered even where the quote denominator legitimately differs.

Capture-attempt completeness and candidate completeness are different denominators. A failed board fetch cannot enumerate unknown contracts. If the eligible frame cannot be enumerated, full-universe profit per eligible candidate is UNKNOWN; report observed-frame metrics explicitly, capture gaps and sensitivity without calling them complete-universe results. A 95% request-success rate does not prove 95% contract capture.

1. **Universe manifest:** manifest_id, schema_version, precommitted_at_utc, collection_start/end, NFL season/week, public game/player identifiers, books/jurisdiction, prop/period/side universe, checkpoints and permissible tolerance, provider/filter scopes, scheduled_requests, policy/version hashes, training_cutoff, public source references, original manifest digest. Freeze before first included observation. Amendments affect future cohorts only. Define base universe separately from analyst-discovered additions.
2. **Capture attempt:** attempt_id, manifest_id, intended_checkpoint, request/response/recorded timestamps, status SUCCESS/EMPTY/PARTIAL/ERROR/DENIED, provider scope, pagination/snapshot completion evidence, count, outage interval, missing_reason. UNKNOWN_BOOK_COVERAGE is different from VERIFIED_NO_OFFER. Never expand account access to fill a gap.
3. **Contract:** contract_id, game_id, player_id, stat unit and provider definition, side predicate, exact threshold, full-game/period, overtime, eligibility, DNP/early-exit/void/push rules, jurisdiction, rule URL/version/digest. Same player/label is insufficient. Distinguish display identity from canonical economic identity, retaining both.
4. **Quote event:** quote_id, contract_id, book/provider IDs, attempt_id, original source timestamp if known, observed_at, recorded_at, expiry, sequence/order evidence, original line/odds, decimal price, active/suspended/unknown state, direct-versus-aggregated evidence, actual access medium, source URL/digest, market-truth confidence, uncertainty. Main/alternate own IDs. New price creates a new event. Source time unknown stays unknown; a fresh board does not repair it.
5. **As-of feature/prediction event:** prediction_id, contract_id, decision_at, feature snapshot hash, each feature published/observed time, model/training/calibration/version hashes, distribution definition, unconditional win/loss/push/void vector or explicitly conditional basis, raw/model/internal/conservative estimates, market/no-vig method, opposing quote IDs, uncertainty method. ## Mathematical replay and worked NFL-prop example

**VERIFIED MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLE:** for a one-unit fixed-odds contract with decimal price d, net payoff is d−1 for WIN, −1 for LOSS, 0 for PUSH/VOID. Expected ROI is p_win(d−1)−p_loss. Fair decimal odds are 1+p_loss/p_win when p_win>0. If using non-push probability, state the conditioning population. Score probabilities on their declared outcome space; do not score unconditional win probability on a push-excluded binary sample.

**MODEL ASSUMPTION — synthetic reception-prop prices, no live offers:** Over4.5 −110 and Under4.5 −110 give implied probabilities 52.38095% each and proportional no-vig Over50%. Raw/model fair probability58%, calibrated/internal57%, conservative53%. Internal probability edge versus break-even4.61905 percentage points, expected ROI8.81818%, conservative ROI1.18182%. Fair odds at57% approximately−132.56; conservative fair odds−112.77. No push assumed. Under an illustrative hard four-point central edge, five-percent expected ROI and strictly positive conservative EV gate, required decimal odds are greater than1/.53=1.886792; the other ROI bound1.05/.57=1.842105 is looser. Therefore juice must be better than−112.76596, not equal at the zero conservative-EV boundary. PRIME's qualified gates remain unchanged. No maximum playable threshold follows from a single-threshold probability. If an additional hypothetical model gives p_internal=.49 and p_conservative=.45 atOver5.5, −110 fails there. Among only these two tested thresholds,4.5 qualifies and5.5 fails; untested thresholds remain unknown.

For policy π with selected set B and complete outcomes, hypothetical ROI=sum(net payoff)/sum(stake). Also report net profit per eligible candidate (including PASS with zero stake/payoff), action rate and all settlement counts. ROI is undefined if no risked units. Report a declared denominator convention for refunded stakes; use submitted one-unit stakes including pushes/voids here and provide nonvoid turnover separately. No weighted policy selection after results.

**Synthetic missingness sensitivity:** a frozen policy selects four −110 contracts. Two observed outcomes are wins; two outcomes are unresolved. Complete-case ROI90.909% is not full-policy ROI. With unit stakes fixed and remaining results bounded between−1 and10/11, total-policy ROI lies between−4.545% and90.909%. Treat unresolved as missing, not a push or a loss; the lower bound is a scenario, not realized loss. If price itself is missing, this bound is unavailable unless a prospective permitted-price cap exists. A narrow interval requires better capture, not optimistic imputation.

**METHODOLOGY_CHALLENGE:** doubly robust estimation is not a repair for a deterministic ticket log with no support for alternative actions. Do not invent logging propensities. Even full information identifies only a specified price-taking hypothetical payoff, assuming no market impact and identical eligibility; it does not identify whether the alternative bet would have been accepted. Repeated quotes and correlated props do not multiply independent football outcomes.

## Falsifiable pilot and acceptance rules

**UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS:** prospective universe capture increases auditability and permits a paired policy comparison that ticket-only selection cannot support. This is a data-quality hypothesis first, not a profitable strategy.

Proposed first stage: two successive NFL weeks for operational capture only, no wagers required. Predeclare a manageable list of permitted books, standard reception/yardage markets and fixed checkpoints; all eligible observations including errors, passes and missing prices retained. Parent provider limits and user permissions control access. Do not promise an exhaustive all-book/all-market universe. This report does not start that collector.

Pilot data acceptance proposal: 100% of scheduled attempts accounted for; 100% of included decision events pass identity/as-of/hash validation; zero future-input leakage or ambiguous settlement in the evaluable subset; at least95% scheduled attempts successful/complete and at least95% selected exact contracts independently outcome-graded by the declared final cutoff. These are proposed engineering tolerances, not statistical proof. Publish all misses, preserve failed pilot weeks, and mark overall inference INCONCLUSIVE when missingness sensitivity reverses a conclusion. An EMPTY provider response without coverage proof does not count as a verified exhaustive success.

After pilot, freeze baseline and challenger, endpoint/practical improvement margin, sample/precision target, training schedule and untouched chronological evaluation weeks. Determine needed weeks from pilot game/week-cluster variance, not arbitrary row counts. Split entire games and keep overlapping player/thesis outcomes together; use week blocks for common shocks and report small-cluster limits. Evaluate paired net profit per eligible candidate first, risked-unit ROI separately, conditional probability calibration/Brier and matched-contract CLV with their own coverage. No profitability declaration from the two-week capture pilot.

Reject data-pipeline readiness if events cannot be reconciled, late quotes/features are admitted, suspensions are ignored, or denominator membership changes after outcomes. Reject an asserted challenger advantage if it disappears under precommitted execution-delay/missingness sensitivity or fails held-out practical-effect/uncertainty criteria. Retain hypotheses that fail. Missing close prevents CLV, not necessarily settlement replay; missing predecision price prevents priced payoff replay. No fix to historic CSVs, current rules, bets or user accounts is part of this deliverable.
