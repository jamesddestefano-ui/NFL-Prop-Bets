## Operating principle

Price player opportunity rather than chase narratives. Role and volume come first; projections and market price come next; matchup is a modifier rather than the starting point. Every recommendation must include a play-to line and maximum acceptable price. A previously good bet becomes a pass when the market moves through the edge.
